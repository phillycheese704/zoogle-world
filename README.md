**🥚🌍 Zoogle World 🌍🥚**

Zoogle World is a browser creature-collection and idle game about hatching eggs, collecting weird little Zoogles, building up your islands, breeding new creatures, discovering mutations and earning increasingly ridiculous amounts of coins and gems.

Everything runs directly in the browser — jump in, hatch something strange, and see what your world turns into.

How to play

Buy an egg from the Market, wait for it to finish in the Hatchery, crack it open and place the Zoogle on an island.

Placed Zoogles generate resources over time. Use those resources to buy better eggs, unlock new areas, upgrade your world, breed creatures together and chase rarer Zoogles.

Some systems unlock later, so the game gradually expands from a simple island and Hatchery into breeding, mutations, Ancient creatures, space areas and multiplayer.

Controls

Mouse / touch

Most of the game is controlled directly through the on-screen UI.

Tap / click buttons, Zoogles and menus to interact

Use the left / right island arrows to move between areas

Tap a Zoogle to open its actions

Use the Market, Index, Quests and other on-screen buttons to open their menus

During placement, tap/click the island to position your Zoogle

Esc closes most open menus and popups on desktop

Gamepad

Zoogle World supports the browser Gamepad API.

D-pad / Left Stick — navigate

A — confirm

B — back / close

X — Collect All

Y — open Index

LB / RB — switch islands

LT / RT — cycle Zoogles

View / Select — open Quests

Menu / Start — open Market

Placement mode also supports controller movement, confirm and cancel.

Main systems

Eggs & Hatchery

Eggs come in different tiers with different prices, hatch times and rarity chances. Higher-tier eggs can reach stronger rarities and more unusual creatures.

Zoogles

Each Zoogle has its own rarity, look, value and resource-generation stats. Some come from eggs, while others are found through breeding, weather, special areas or other systems.

Breeding Cave

Choose two Zoogles and breed them together. Certain combinations have special results, while other pairings produce creatures based on the parents’ rarities.

Mutations

Weather and experiments can give Zoogles permanent mutations with stat bonuses and unique visual effects. Mutations can stack, making individual Zoogles increasingly unusual.

Weather

Weather can temporarily change the world and sometimes create mutation opportunities. Some weather is common, while rarer events can lead to much harder-to-find mutations.

Storage Cliffs

Keep Zoogles safely stored away when you do not want them placed on an island.

Tribal Island

A more ancient part of Zoogle World, with its own progression and Ancient creature content.

Moon & Space Merchant

The Moon introduces the Space Merchant, rotating stock, special eggs, Totems and alien-themed content.

Mothership

A late-game system area containing upgrade controls for systems such as Hatchery, Breeding and Storage capacity.

Multiplayer & visiting

Visit other worlds, share your island, send join requests and play together in a shared world session.

Saves

Your progress is saved automatically in the browser. Avoid clearing site data or using Reset Save unless you actually want to start over.
